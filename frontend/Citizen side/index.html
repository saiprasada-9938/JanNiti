ABOUT THE INITIATIVE

Building better communities through citizen participation.

JanNiti is a citizen-focused digital platform designed to provide a simple and accessible way for people to communicate development-related problems in their communities.

The platform organizes citizen complaints by category, location and urgency so that important community concerns can be identified and given appropriate attention.
